Questions

Still have questions?

Is Dinnerkind free?

Yes. Dinnerkind is free to download on iPhone, with no sign-up and no ads. There’s also an optional Dinnerkind Pro subscription inside the app, which comes with a 1-week free trial.

Do I need to make an account?

No. There’s no sign-up. Just pick how many people you cook for and any food allergies, and your week is ready.

Can it leave out food allergies?

Yes. Pick from common allergies like eggs, fish, gluten, milk, peanuts, sesame, shellfish, soy, tree nuts and wheat, or add your own, and dinners that have them are left out. Dinnerkind goes by each recipe’s ingredients, and brands vary, so always check labels.

What if I don’t like one of the dinners?

Swap any night you want. You can also swap a single ingredient, like feta for goat cheese, and your grocery list updates too.

What if we’re eating out some nights?

Skip any night. On This Week, press and hold a dinner and tap Skip This Night. It becomes a night off, and its groceries come off your list, so you only shop for the dinners you’ll cook.

How does it help me waste less food?

Your dinners share groceries. When a dinner opens something, like a bunch of parsley or a bag of spinach, the rest is planned into another dinner within 2 days, so it gets used while it’s fresh.

How long do the dinners take?

Most are ready in 40 minutes or less, and every dinner shows its cook time before you start.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Dinnerkind is available on iPhone from the App Store.
